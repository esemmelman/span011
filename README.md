# Ser and estar together

A beginning-level Spanish practice app patterned after `span010`. The home page links to a conjugation guide, a selection-based conjugation quiz, sentence examples, and two exercises. The conjugation guide has hover and focus rules and a 45-second study timer that locks controls across the app until it ends. The sentence examples show ten mixed uses of **ser** and **estar**. Exercise 1 offers the two verb forms for the subject. Exercise 2 asks learners to build each Spanish sentence from three word columns with at most five choices each. The quiz and exercises score answers and offer a fresh set.

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step or dependencies are required. The GitHub Pages workflow publishes changes pushed to `main`.
